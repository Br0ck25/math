## Study (school subjects for grades 6-8)
- Study (`38-study.js`) is not a popup: it takes the place of the arena and keypad on the main page (`#study-pane`, `studyOn`, `.study-on`), switched by the bottom-bar Math/Study button (`toggleStudy()`; Settings also has a link). While it is on there are no math keys and the math question clock is paused. **Study answers fight like math answers**: a right answer calls `triggerMonsterDamage` and a wrong one `triggerBuddyDamage` (or uses a shield), via `studyBattle()`, so damage, status, fainting (`studyBlocked()`: a fainted fighter must be replaced first), foe victory and loot all work the same; only typed and multiple-choice answers fight (flashcards are self-marked). The per-answer gems and XP of math are not paid for study answers: the study round pays its own capped amount. Popups use `#study-popup` while the math area is hidden. **Study is trivia mode: it changes the whole game** (`studyOn`, `body.trivia`), while buddies, bosses and progress stay: `makeMcq()` returns a study question (`studyMcq()`, same shape plus `.study`) so the Cup, the same-room duel and the vault ask study questions (compare answers with `mcqRight()`, record with `mcqRecord()`, show with `fmtAns()`); online duels carry them as `{t:'t', e, l:'', o, a}` which the server checks in `cleanQuestions` (plain text, shown only through `escHtml`) and scores in `/api/duel/submit`; a parent's own questions are never used outside the player's own battles; `triviaWords()` turns "Number Kingdom" into "Trivia Kingdom" (map title, story windows); math-only settings (`.math-only`) hide and `.trivia-only` ones show; a boss's weak point becomes a school subject (`studyWeakSubject()`); the practice chip shows the subjects. The player can tick several subjects (`state.study.subjects`; none = all). It has three modes: flashcards (self-marked, never pays), Quick Recall (multiple choice) and Type it. Subjects and the question validator are in `src/shared/study.js` (`STUDY_SUBJECTS`, `cleanStudyQ()`, used by the page and the Worker); numbers are in `ECON.study`.
- Questions live in `37-study-data.js` (`studyAdd(subject, rows, idKey)`) and one file per source. **The bank holds only KAAC practice questions**, word for word, with KAAC's written permission (the owner has it): `37f-study-kaac.js` (2024 elementary district arts test, ids `kaac1`...), `37g-study-kaac25-assess.js` (2025 middle grades district content assessments, multiple choice, ids `assess1`...) and `37h-study-kaac25-quick.js` (2025 district Quick Recall rounds 1-6 and overtime, ids `quick1`...). Topics start with `kaac`, and the Study screen credits KAAC when they are in the mix. Every question that comes with a picture, map, graph or table is left out, and so are the math questions whose figures or formulas the text cannot carry. The owner removed the 543 questions written for the first version, so never write questions of your own into the bank, and never paste any other test's questions without the owner saying they have permission. (The quick recall rounds do not print a subject: each block of five holds one of each, so it was worked out from the content.) Rows are `[level 6-8, topic, question, answer, "alt|alt", explanation, "wrong|wrong|wrong"]`. **Only append rows to a file; never reorder or delete** (ids are key + position and saves refer to them; loading a save forgets ids that are no longer in the bank). The subject buttons and the Kentucky and level rows only show when there is something in them. Typed answers go through `studyCheck()` (case, "the", commas, spaces and one slip in a long word are fine), so list real alternatives in `alt`. Wrong choices for multiple choice come from `studyChoices()` (nearby numbers, then other answers on the same topic); give a row its own `wrong` list when it is a fill-in-the-blank or yes/no. `tests/study.test.mjs` checks every row.
- Rewards are deliberately small: a right answer pays only if that question has not paid in `ECON.study.freshDays`, at most `ECON.study.dayCap` gems a day, flashcards pay nothing. Study time counts toward the daily time goal; answers go in the parent report as `st` and `sub` (`studyStat()`, `studyParts()`) and the questions answered wrong, with how many times, as `studyMissed` (`kidSummary()`, shown by `studyMissedHtml()` on the parent page and in the child's own report); flashcards are not reported. Missed questions are kept in `state.study.miss` and are picked first.
- A parent adds questions on the parent page (`35b-parent-study.js`, `src/server/17-study.js`): one form, any of the children the parent is first parent of ("All my children" or a choice), or many lines at once. Key `pq:<child>` is written only by the primary parent and counts only while that link is still primary (like `ctl:`); the child's device fetches `GET /api/study`, caches it per player name (`setParentQs()`), and shows it only to that player. A parent can also switch a child to "only my questions" (`only` flag on `pq:<child>`, `studyOnlyParent()`): the built-in questions and the level row are hidden while the parent has questions for that child. Limits are in `ECON.study.parent`. Parent questions are never used in duels, tournaments or the vault, and every string goes through `escHtml()` when shown.
