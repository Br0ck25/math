## The Adventure battle (24w-ow-battle-data.js, 24x-ow-battle.js, 24y-ow-arenas.js)
- The game is the Adventure map. Every fight is a Pokemon Gold style battle on `#ab-modal`. There is no Practice screen, no keypad and no parent dashboard any more.
- A turn: FIGHT asks a math question (`abQuestion()`: the adaptive problem from `07b-adaptive.js`, or a study question in trivia mode). A right answer lets you pick an attack; a wrong one is a miss. Then the foe attacks. PACK (Catch Net on wild buddies, Potion), BUDDY (swap) and RUN (wild only) work as in Gold.
- Everything that happens is a queue of steps `[text, run, ms]` (`abSteps`, `abNow`); menus open only when the queue is empty. Add a new effect as steps, never as timers.
- Numbers follow Gold (checked against pret/pokegold): damage `abDamage` (physical = Normal moves, special = every element, crit 17/256, STAB, type chart, 217-255 luck), stats from base + a per-species shape (`abShape`), experience `floor(floor(base/fighters)*level/7)` (x1.5 trainers) with level cubed growth (`xpForLevel`), the catch rule `abCatchA`, sleep 2-7 turns, burn/poison 1/8, paralysis quarters Speed, priority then Speed decides order, a trainer's buddy picks the best move, blackout halves your gems. Time of day uses Gold's hours (`owTimeOfDay`).
- Battle HP and PP are saved per buddy (`state.advHp`, `state.advPp`); the Buddy Center restores them. Moves are chosen in `state.advMoves` (the newest four by default; a full buddy is asked which to forget; the Move Tutor relearns). A buddy with 0 HP is resting (`isSleepy`).
- A gym you walk into (`24vp-ow-gyms.js`: its trainers stand along the way, a math switch door shuts in the leader in two of three layouts) ends with the leader's fight as one round (`abStartGym(id, true)`); a Champion's rematch is still a chain of rounds fought with the same team (`abGymRounds`), the Hall is the Elite Four (five buddies each) then Nova's six. Money follows Gold: only trainers pay, `abPrize` = class base (`ECON.money`: 16, or 100 for leaders, the Elite Four, the Champion and the Master) times the level of the last buddy; wild buddies, level-ups, badges and story beats pay nothing. `advanceGym()` gives the badge and `championReward()` the title. A boss or leader beaten runs `abBossBeaten` after the screen closes (Dex book, Alpha wins, rare drop, quests).
- Tests that need a fight to end quickly: set every foe's `hp = 1`, `atk = 1` and `spc = 1`, stub `Math.random = () => 0.3` (every move lands, no status, no crit), answer with `abAnswer(ab.q.ans)` and use `abUseMove(0)`. A buddy that levels up may ask to forget a move (`ab.phase === 'learn'`): answer with `abLearnChoose(-1)`.
- Wild buddies live in `13b-wild.js` (`rollWildSpecies`, weather, time of day). Spares are sold with `sellSpare()`. Eggs (`13c-eggs.js`) hatch as you walk and win. The buddy hub (`25-buddy-hub.js`: held items and duels) is reached from the Menu.
